Selling two identical apartments in one building helps both owners, as long as each is handled on its own terms.

  1. Each price guide is set on its own evidence

    Your level, your outlook and your letting history, not the other apartment's.

  2. Offers stay confidential

    Neither owner hears the other's offers or prices. What you accept is between you and your buyer.

  3. Buyers see both, and choose

    Every buyer whose brief fits is shown both apartments. A buyer who misses one is still a buyer for the other.

  4. Your feedback is yours alone

    Buyer feedback and offers on your apartment go to you only, and the same holds the other way.

What I need from you

Since 1 August 2025, a Queensland seller must give the buyer a signed disclosure statement and the prescribed certificates before the contract is signed. I coordinate it with your solicitor in week 1, so a buyer can sign the day they decide.

  • Signed appointment (Form 6)
  • Photo ID for the AML/CTF check
  • Your solicitor or conveyancer, for the Form 2 disclosure statement
  • Body corporate details and the latest levy notice
  • Letting agreement and the last 12 months of income statements
  • Dates kept free of guests for the shoot and inspections

Before you decide

Will the apartment keep earning while it is for sale?

That is the aim. The shoot and the inspections are booked with the on-site manager around guest stays, so the apartment stays in the letting pool between them.

What if it does not sell?

You pay no commission. The only cost is the marketing you chose in section 04, paid before launch.

How long does it take?

One week of private preview while the campaign is prepared, then the public launch. After that it depends on the market and on the offer you choose to accept.

Do I need to be there?

No. I handle access, inspections and buyers. Documents can be signed electronically.

Why not sell it quietly, off-market?

The preview week gives you the quiet part first. If a Qube buyer pays what it is worth, it can end there. If not, the public launch brings the rest of the market in, so the price is tested properly.

Who decides the commission?

You do, within the band above, before settlement, once you have seen the campaign and the result.
