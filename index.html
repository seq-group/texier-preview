The making, in four movementsSkip01/ 04
I

Chosen from the remnant.

Italian and French mills cut cloth for fashion houses. What remains of the roll reaches the atelier in Bordeaux, in the south-west of France, where it is chosen for its hand and character.

II

Cut and matched by hand.

Each piece is cut from its own pattern by the hands that will stitch it, the weave aligned across every seam.

III

Finished in leather.

A leather collar, saddle-stitched. It deepens with every round and, in time, takes on its own patina.

IV

Numbered as it is made.

Camille numbers each piece by hand when she finishes it, and writes the number on the card in the box. The number is never chosen: it belongs to the piece.

Maison Texier is a meeting of French heritage, considered craftsmanship and the timeless elegance of golf.

With family roots in New Caledonia and a home on Australia's Gold Coast, the Maison was founded by Cyril Texier with a desire to bring a more refined expression of craftsmanship to the game.

At the heart of Maison Texier is Camille Texier, whose work in Bordeaux, France, embodies the traditions of French artisanal excellence. Trained in the world of fine leather goods, Camille brings an intimate understanding of materials, proportion and finishing to every creation.

The Maison's first collection celebrates an often-overlooked object: the golf headcover. Each piece is individually made, with the same consideration given to its construction as to its appearance.

With time, the leather acquires its own patina, the materials soften and each piece develops a character inseparable from its owner. Produced in limited quantities and individually numbered, Maison Texier creations are intended to be kept, used and appreciated for years to come.
